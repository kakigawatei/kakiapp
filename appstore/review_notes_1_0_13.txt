[Version 1.0.13 — build 27]

This is a small update. No new permissions, no new data collection.

- School team contest: if a user checks in at the shop and then joins a school team on the same day, that day's visit is now counted for the team (once per day).
- Leaving a school team and deleting the account are now done in a single server transaction, so the team member count and the user's record stay consistent. Account deletion (My Page > Account) removes the member data.
- My Page has a new card with links to the official website, the shops' Instagram accounts and the shops' Google review pages. They open outside the app (external browser / app). There is no reward for writing a review.
- Location is used only when the user opens the app or taps "check in", to confirm they are near the shop; coordinates are not stored or transmitted.
