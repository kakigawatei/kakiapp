[Version 1.0.1 — build 8]

This is a feature update to version 1.0, which is already live on the App Store.

What changed in this build:

1) Added our second restaurant location (Sendai Renbo). Version 1.0 only recognized the Nagaoka main store, so customers visiting our Sendai restaurant could not earn visit points. Both locations now work.

2) Reworked the opening screen. The app now shows the customer's current rank and total visit count first, instead of the point balance.

3) Expanded the rank system from 4 tiers to 6 tiers, and added an animation when the customer is promoted to a new rank.

4) Replaced the visit-gacha and daily-roulette animations with new ones.

5) The header showing the point balance is now fixed while scrolling.

6) Added an opt-in checkbox for promotional email at sign-up, and a switch on My Page so the customer can turn it off at any time. This is required under Japan's Act on Regulation of Transmission of Specified Electronic Mail.

How to test:
- Sign in with the demo account below. The account is already email-verified.
- The rank card appears immediately after sign-in.
- "毎日ルーレット" (daily roulette) on the Home tab can be spun once per day without any location requirement.
- The visit gacha requires GPS proximity to one of our restaurants, so it cannot be tested from outside Japan. Please use the daily roulette to confirm the point-earning flow.
- Account deletion is available at My Page > アカウントを削除する (as reviewed and approved in version 1.0).
