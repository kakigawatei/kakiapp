[Version 1.0.4 — build 12]

This update adds an in-app "Announcements" panel (bell icon at the top-left of the home screen) and fixes several UI issues:
- Double-tap no longer zooms the page (viewport / touch-action).
- The header logo image is now bundled with the app (it was missing from the native bundle in 1.0.3).
- The point-redemption keypad now has a labeled "Clear" (クリア) button and a larger backspace button.
- Line breaks in the hero copy and card titles are tidied.

Announcements are read-only public documents fetched from Firestore over HTTPS; no new permissions are requested. If the shop has not posted any announcement yet, the panel shows "いまはお知らせはありません" (no announcements right now).
