[Resubmission: build 1.0 (7) — fixes for the two issues raised on 2026-08-31]

1) Guideline 2.1(a) — Sign in with Google / Apple did not complete on iPad.
   Both third-party sign-in buttons have been removed. The app now offers email/password sign-in only.
   Additionally, Firebase Auth is now initialized with the native-app path (initializeAuth + IndexedDB persistence), so the sign-in screen appears reliably on iPhone and iPad.

2) Guideline 5.1.1(v) — Account deletion.
   Added "アカウントを削除する" (Delete account) inside the app:
   My Page tab (bottom right "マイページ") → "アカウント" section → red button "アカウントを削除する" → two confirmation dialogs → the account (Firebase Auth user) and all of the user's data (Firestore document) are deleted → the app returns to the sign-in screen.
   No support contact is required; deletion is completed in-app immediately.
   Screen recording of the full deletion flow (sign-in → My Page → delete → 2 confirmations → sign-in screen): see the attached video kakiapp_delete_demo.mp4 (41 s).
