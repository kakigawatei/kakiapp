[Version 1.0.6 — build 14]

This is a small feature update on top of 1.0.5 (approved on Sep 11, 2026):
- Time-limited point gifts ("campaigns") that the shop owner sets from a separate admin page. When a campaign is active, eligible users receive the points once on launch, with a small celebration message and a bell notification. The first one is our 6th-anniversary gift (Oct 7–13, 2026).
- A one-time bonus when a user's rank goes up.
- Minor fixes.

No new permissions, no new SDKs. Data is read from the same Firebase project as before (one additional read-only collection for campaigns).
