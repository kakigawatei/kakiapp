[Version 1.0.2 — build 10]

This is a feature and design update to version 1.0.1, which is already live on the App Store.

What changed in this build:

1) Self-service point redemption. Previously the customer had to ask a staff member to redeem points. Now the customer redeems points inside the app ("つかう" tab) and a redemption screen with a code appears; the customer simply shows that screen when ordering. No staff-side app or hardware is involved.

2) Full visual redesign: white background, new icons, redesigned home, roulette, gacha and rank screens. No functional change to point rules.

3) Home screen copy now explains what the app does (earn points per visit, exchange for a bowl).

4) The fraud warning on the gacha screen was consolidated into My Page (it was shown twice before).

How to test:
- Sign in with the demo account below. The account is already email-verified.
- Home shows rank and visit count. "つかう" shows the redemption flow; tapping the exchange button opens the redemption screen (points are deducted from the demo account, which is fine).
- Location check-in requires being at one of our restaurants, so the visit gacha cannot be triggered from outside; the roulette can be played daily from anywhere.
- Please do not delete the demo account (the "アカウント削除" button on My Page), as reviewers would then be unable to sign in.
